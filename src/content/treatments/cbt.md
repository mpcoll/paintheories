## Overview

CBT has become a first-line psychosocial treatment for chronic pain, with formats adapted to children, older adults, telephone and web delivery [@ehde2014].

## Evidence

The 2020 Cochrane review is the largest synthesis: across 59 CBT trials, mostly in fibromyalgia, chronic low back pain, rheumatoid arthritis and mixed chronic pain, CBT gave small benefits for pain, disability and distress compared with usual care, largely maintained at follow-up, and very small benefits compared with other active treatments [@williams2020].

## Open questions

- Which people benefit most, and through which mechanisms, is still poorly understood.
- Trials rarely blind participants or measure expectations, which complicates comparisons with active controls [@williams2020].
