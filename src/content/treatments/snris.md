## Overview

SNRIs such as duloxetine and venlafaxine were developed as antidepressants. Their pain-relieving effect appears at doses that do not depend on lifting mood, and duloxetine is among the best-studied drugs for painful diabetic neuropathy [@lunn2014].

## Evidence

For neuropathic pain as a whole, the 2025 NeuPSIG meta-analysis estimates that about 7 people need to be treated for one more to gain at least 50% pain relief than with placebo [@soliman2025]. Effects in fibromyalgia are of similar or smaller size and rest on lower-quality evidence, and most trials were run or sponsored by the manufacturer [@lunn2014].

## In practice

Guidelines place SNRIs early for neuropathic pain [@soliman2025] and as one option among antidepressants for chronic primary pain [@nice2021]. Benefit is modest for most people, so trials of treatment with clear stopping rules are common.
