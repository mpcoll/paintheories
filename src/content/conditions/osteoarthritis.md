## Overview

Osteoarthritis is the most common form of arthritis in adults. It mainly affects the knee, hip and hand, usually after the age of 40, and its prevalence rises steeply with age [@steinmetz2023]. Chronic pain and loss of mobility are its defining burdens.

## How it is classified

ICD-11 codes osteoarthritis pain as **chronic secondary musculoskeletal pain associated with structural changes**, alongside the code for the affected joint [@who2026; @perrot2019]. The pain is treated as a symptom of an identified disease, in contrast to non-specific chronic low back pain.

## Mechanisms

Pain originates in persistent nociception from the joint and surrounding structures [@perrot2019]. In a subgroup of people, hypersensitivity of the central nervous system makes an important additional contribution [@lluch2014].

## Open questions

- Which features identify the subgroup with a strong central contribution, and does it predict poorer outcomes after surgery?
- How joint inflammation, structural change and nervous-system changes interact over the course of the disease.
