## Overview

In a classic experiment, trains of noxious heat pulses to the hand made first pain fade but second pain grow with each pulse when pulses came every 3 s or faster [@price1977]. Because the summation persisted when first pain was blocked and when the stimulus site changed, it was attributed to central mechanisms, in line with the summated responses of wide dynamic range dorsal horn neurons to repeated C-fibre input [@price1977]. Temporal summation tests are now part of standard QST as the wind-up ratio [@rolke2006].

## In research and practice

Enhanced temporal summation is used as a sign of central facilitation in chronic pain, together with widespread sensitization and reduced descending modulation [@arendtnielsen2017]. People with fibromyalgia show more temporal summation than controls [@staud2001; @obrien2018]. Protocols vary widely, which adds heterogeneity between studies [@obrien2018], and the wind-up ratio is one of the less reliable QST parameters [@geber2011]. In one treatment study in painful diabetic neuropathy, temporal summation did not predict response to duloxetine, whereas CPM did [@yarnitsky2012].
