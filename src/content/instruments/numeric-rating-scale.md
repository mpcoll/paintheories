## Overview

The 0 to 10 numeric rating scale is the simplest and most widely used pain measure: one question about intensity, usually "in the last 24 hours" or "right now". It can be given in person, by phone, on paper or in an electronic diary.

## Strengths and limits

Compared with visual analogue and verbal rating scales it usually has better completion rates and corresponds well with VAS scores, although many comparison studies express no preference [@hjermstad2011]. Anchor wording and the number of points still vary between versions. It captures only intensity, so it is paired with measures of function, mood and global change in trials [@dworkin2005]. What counts as a meaningful change depends on the starting level, which is why relative (percentage) changes are often reported [@farrar2001].
