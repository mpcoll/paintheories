## Overview

A pressure algometer has a probe of fixed area that the examiner presses on the skin at a steady rate until the person says the pressure has become painful. It is one of the mechanical pain thresholds in the DFNS quantitative sensory testing protocol [@rolke2006] and is also used on its own because it is quick and portable.

## Strengths and limits

With training in a fixed application rate, different raters obtained highly consistent thresholds in healthy people [@chesterton2007]. Thresholds measured at the painful region and at distant sites are used to separate local from widespread sensitization in chronic pain [@arendtnielsen2017]. Thresholds depend on the site tested, age and sex [@rolke2006], and on how the test is done, so a value means little without matched reference data or a within-person comparison.
