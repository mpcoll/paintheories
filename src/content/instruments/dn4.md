## Overview

The French Neuropathic Pain Group developed the DN4 by comparing the descriptors and bedside sensory signs of patients with definite nerve lesions and patients with somatic lesions, and found that a small number of items was enough to tell them apart [@bouhassira2005]. The interview items cover qualities such as burning, painful cold and electric shocks and sensations such as tingling, pins and needles, numbness and itching; the examination items test for reduced touch and pinprick sensation and for pain evoked by brushing [@bouhassira2005].

## Strengths and limits

The DN4 is quick and among the best-supported neuropathic pain screening tools [@mathieson2015]. Like the other tools, it identifies likely neuropathic pain rather than diagnosing it: screening tools miss about 10% to 20% of patients with clinician-diagnosed neuropathic pain and do not replace clinical judgment [@bennett2007; @mathieson2015].
