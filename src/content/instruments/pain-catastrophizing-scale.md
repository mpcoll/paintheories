## Overview

The PCS asks how often people have particular thoughts and feelings when they are in pain, such as being unable to stop thinking about it or feeling that it is terrible and will never get better. It was developed and first validated by Sullivan and colleagues [@sullivan1995], and later confirmed in adult community and clinical samples [@osman2000].

## In research and practice

Catastrophizing is central to the fear-avoidance model and a frequent target of cognitive behavioural treatment; the PCS is the usual way to measure it. Scores differ between community and clinical samples and between women and men [@osman2000], so interpreting a score needs the right comparison group.
