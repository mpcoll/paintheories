## Overview

Waddell and colleagues built the FABQ from theories of fear and avoidance, focusing on what patients believe about how physical activity and work affect their back pain [@waddell1993]. The two subscales are scored separately rather than as one total.

## Strengths and limits

In the development study, beliefs about work were strongly related to work loss even after allowing for pain severity, which made the work subscale of particular interest in occupational settings [@waddell1993]. The questionnaire is specific to back pain and to beliefs, not to fear as an emotion, so it complements rather than replaces measures such as the Tampa Scale for Kinesiophobia.
