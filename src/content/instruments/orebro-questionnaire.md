## Overview

The full Örebro questionnaire has 25 items and is also known as the Acute Low Back Pain Screening Questionnaire. Linton, Nicholas and MacDonald built the short form by taking two items from each of the five factors with predictive power, and tested it against the long form in occupational and primary care samples [@linton2011].

## In research and practice

The short form predicted sick leave almost as well as the long form, and a cut-off of 50 picked up most people who went on to develop work disability [@linton2011]. It was validated against sick leave, not pain or function, and the developers' scoring guide describes a score above 50 as indicating higher estimated risk of future work disability. The developer's FAQ on the full questionnaire adds that it was not designed to evaluate treatment outcome.
