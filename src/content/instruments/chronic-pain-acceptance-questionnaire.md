## Overview

In the psychological flexibility model, acceptance means reducing unsuccessful attempts to avoid or control pain and engaging in valued activities instead [@mccracken2004; @mccracken2014]. McCracken and colleagues refined the CPAQ to the two components that held up, activity engagement and pain willingness [@mccracken2004]. Fish and colleagues derived the CPAQ-8 from an online mixed chronic pain sample [@fish2010].

## In research and practice

Higher acceptance is associated with less distress and interference, and the two subscales partly mediated the effect of pain severity on interference and emotional distress in the CPAQ-8 study, with activity engagement more strongly related to outcomes than pain willingness [@fish2010]. A systematic review judged that no acceptance questionnaire met every quality criterion, although the CPAQ had the most accumulated evidence [@reneman2010].
