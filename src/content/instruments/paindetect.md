## Overview

painDETECT was developed with the German Research Network on Neuropathic Pain as a patient-completed screen that needs no clinical examination [@freynhagen2006; @bennett2007]. Its total score is usually read in three bands: 12 or less means a neuropathic component is unlikely, 19 or more that it is likely, and scores in between are uncertain [@sachau2023].

## In research and practice

In the development survey, 37% of an unselected cohort of people with chronic low back pain had predominantly neuropathic pain by this measure, and they reported more intense pain and more depression, anxiety and sleep problems [@freynhagen2006]. A positive score indicates neuropathic-like symptoms, not a confirmed lesion or disease of the somatosensory nervous system, so it supports rather than replaces clinical assessment [@bennett2007; @mathieson2015].
