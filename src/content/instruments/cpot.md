## Overview

Many patients in intensive care cannot say how much pain they have because they are sedated, intubated or confused. The CPOT asks the nurse to score four observable behaviours. In its validation study, patients were assessed at rest, during repositioning and 20 minutes later, while unconscious and intubated, conscious and intubated, and after extubation [@gelinas2006].

## Strengths and limits

Scores rose during a nociceptive procedure whatever the patient's level of consciousness and were associated with patients' own reports when they could give them [@gelinas2006]. The validation sample came from cardiac surgery in a single centre, so other critical care groups, such as people with brain injury, need their own evidence. A behaviour score is an observer's judgement, not the patient's own rating.
