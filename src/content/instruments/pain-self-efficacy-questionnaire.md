## Overview

Self-efficacy is a person's confidence that they can carry out a behaviour. Nicholas argued that for people with chronic pain the relevant belief is confidence in doing things despite the pain, and the PSEQ asks about exactly that [@nicholas2007]. Its properties were first established in chronic low back pain and later confirmed in a large mixed chronic pain cohort attending a pain management programme [@nicholas2007].

## In research and practice

Self-efficacy is a core process in cognitive behavioural accounts of chronic pain, and the PSEQ can be used to assess people before and after treatment [@nicholas2007]. A COSMIN-based review found good reliability and responsiveness, with meaningful-change values reported for chronic low back pain only, and called for responsiveness studies in other groups [@dube2021].
