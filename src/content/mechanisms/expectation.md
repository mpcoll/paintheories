---
title: Expectation
summary: Anticipated outcomes, formed by verbal information, prior experience, observation or conditioning, that shape the pain actually experienced; expectations of relief reduce pain (placebo analgesia) and expectations of harm increase it (nocebo hyperalgesia).
levels: [computational, systems]
references: [kirsch1985, atlas2012]
---
Expectation effects on pain engage prefrontal, opioidergic descending and, in some studies, spinal mechanisms. Whether conditioning acts directly or through the expectations it creates is debated.
