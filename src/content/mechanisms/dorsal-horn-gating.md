---
title: Dorsal horn gating
summary: Modulation of nociceptive transmission in the spinal dorsal horn by concurrent large-fibre input and by descending control from the brain.
levels: [circuit]
references: [melzack1965, mendell2014]
---
The spinal "gate" proposed by Melzack and Wall. The specific circuit they proposed (presynaptic inhibition in the substantia gelatinosa) was not confirmed in its original form, but segmental inhibition of dorsal horn projection neurons by non-nociceptive input is well established.
