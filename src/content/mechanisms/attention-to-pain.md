---
title: Attention to pain (capture and control)
summary: Nociceptive and pain-signalling stimuli involuntarily capture attention and interrupt ongoing activity (bottom-up), while current goals, working memory and attentional set can limit that capture (top-down); hypervigilance and distraction are two ends of this interplay.
levels: [computational, systems]
references: [eccleston1999, legrain2009, vandamme2010]
---
Attention is central to the interruptive function, neurocognitive and motivational accounts of attention to pain, and the salience account of pain-related brain responses. Distraction-induced analgesia partly involves descending inhibition at the spinal level.
