---
title: Stress response and allostasis
summary: Neuroendocrine (hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal axis), autonomic, immune and metabolic responses to stressors, which are adaptive in the short term but impose cumulative "allostatic load" on brain and body when repeated or prolonged.
levels: [systems, cellular]
references: [mcewen1998, mcewen2010]
---
Stress mediators such as cortisol act on hippocampal, amygdala and prefrontal circuits that also regulate pain. Altered stress-axis function has been reported in several chronic pain conditions, but findings are inconsistent in direction and in whether they precede pain.
