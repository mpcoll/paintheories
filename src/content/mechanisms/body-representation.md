---
title: Body representation
summary: Multisensory cortical representations of the body and the space around it (body schema, body image, sense of ownership) that integrate touch, proprioception, vision and motor signals, and whose disruption is linked to altered body perception in some chronic pain conditions.
levels: [systems, computational]
references: [moseley2012, moseley2008c, harris1999]
---
Disturbances of body representation (altered perceived size or shape of a painful limb, reduced tactile acuity, spatially defined deficits in tactile processing) are reported in complex regional pain syndrome, phantom limb pain and chronic back pain. Whether they cause pain, follow from it, or both, is unresolved.
