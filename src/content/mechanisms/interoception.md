---
title: Interoception
summary: Sensing, representing and regulating the physiological condition of the body (temperature, pain, itch, visceral and muscular state), with cortical representation centred on the insula.
levels: [circuit, systems]
references: [craig2002]
---
Craig proposed that small-diameter afferents and lamina I spinothalamic neurons form a dedicated interoceptive pathway to the posterior and anterior insula, and that pain is one of the feelings this system generates. Whether pain is best classed with interoceptive or exteroceptive senses is debated.
