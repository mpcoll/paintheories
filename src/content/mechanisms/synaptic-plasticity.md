---
title: Activity-dependent synaptic plasticity
summary: Use-dependent increases in the efficacy of nociceptive synapses in the spinal cord and brain that amplify responses to subsequent input.
levels: [cellular, circuit]
references: [woolf2000, latremoliere2009]
---
Includes wind-up, long-term potentiation at nociceptive synapses, and heterosynaptic facilitation. These processes are the cellular basis of central sensitization.
