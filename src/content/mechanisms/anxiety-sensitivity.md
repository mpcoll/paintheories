---
title: Anxiety sensitivity
summary: A dispositional fear of anxiety-related bodily sensations, based on beliefs that they have harmful physical, psychological or social consequences; proposed as a vulnerability for both pain-related fear and anxiety disorders.
levels: [psychosocial]
references: [ocanez2010, asmundson2009]
---
Anxiety sensitivity is associated most strongly with fearful appraisals of pain, and more modestly with pain tolerance, threshold and pain-related disability [@ocanez2010]. It is the central shared vulnerability factor in models of co-occurring chronic pain and anxiety disorders such as PTSD [@asmundson2009].
