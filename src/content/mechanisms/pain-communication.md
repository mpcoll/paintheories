---
title: Pain communication
summary: The expression of pain through verbal report and non-verbal behaviour (notably facial expression), its decoding by observers, and the social responses this elicits.
levels: [psychosocial, sociocultural]
references: [hadjistavropoulos2002, craig2009, hadjistavropoulos2011]
---
Pain expression ranges from relatively automatic behaviour, such as facial expression, to self-report that is under greater voluntary control; observers decode both, with characteristic biases, and their responses in turn shape the person's pain and behaviour [@hadjistavropoulos2002; @hadjistavropoulos2011].
