---
title: Descending modulation
summary: Facilitation and inhibition of spinal nociceptive transmission by brainstem and forebrain circuits (e.g. periaqueductal grey, rostral ventromedial medulla).
levels: [circuit, systems]
references: [wiech2016]
---
Descending pathways allow cognitive and emotional states (expectation, attention, fear, placebo) to change nociceptive signalling before it reaches the brain. They are a central component of gate control, motivation-decision, and predictive accounts of pain.
