---
title: Distributed brain network
summary: Pain-related activity spread across many cortical and subcortical regions (somatosensory, insular, cingulate, prefrontal, thalamic) rather than a single "pain centre".
levels: [systems]
references: [iannetti2010]
---
The idea that pain arises from a widely distributed network underlies the neuromatrix concept and the later "pain matrix" used in neuroimaging. Whether the network is specific to pain, or reflects the detection of salient events more generally, is debated.
