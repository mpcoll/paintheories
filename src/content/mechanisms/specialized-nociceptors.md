---
title: Specialized nociceptors
summary: Primary afferent neurons that respond selectively to stimuli that damage or threaten to damage tissue (thermal, mechanical, chemical).
levels: [cellular, circuit]
references: [perl2007]
---
The existence of primary afferents tuned specifically to noxious stimuli was disputed for decades and was only firmly established with single-unit recordings in the 1960s (Burgess and Perl; Bessou and Perl). Their discovery is often cited as partial vindication of specificity theory, although nociceptor activity is neither necessary nor sufficient for pain.
