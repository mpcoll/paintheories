---
title: Reward and motivational circuitry
summary: Mesolimbic and corticostriatal circuits (ventral tegmental area, nucleus accumbens, ventromedial prefrontal cortex, amygdala) that compute value, prediction errors and motivation, including the value of pain and of pain relief.
levels: [systems, behavioural]
references: [baliki2015, borsook2016]
---
Pain relief is rewarding and pain suppresses reward-seeking. Several accounts of chronic pain propose that persistent pain alters these circuits, or that their pre-existing properties determine whether pain persists.
