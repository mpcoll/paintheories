## Overview

The model applies McEwen's concept of allostatic load to pain. Allostasis is "stability through change": the brain and body continually adjust stress mediators to meet demands, and allostatic load is the cost of repeated, prolonged or inadequately terminated responses [@mcewen1993; @mcewen1998]. Borsook, Maleki, Becerra and McEwen argued that migraine is a model disease of allostatic load: repeated attacks act as stressors, and maladaptive responses to them change brain structure and function, increasing attack frequency and comorbidity [@borsook2012].

Extended to chronic pain generally, the model treats pain as both a stressor and a consequence of a brain made vulnerable by cumulative stress. Corticosteroids and other stress mediators act on hippocampal, amygdala and prefrontal circuits that also regulate pain, providing a route from stress to pain persistence [@mcewen2010]. Later work added reward deficiency and "anti-reward" processes as an allostatic shift in motivational circuits [@borsook2016], and recent statements frame allostatic load in pain as an instability of a combined biological and social system [@borsook2025].

## Historical context

The idea links two traditions: stress physiology (Selye; McEwen's allostasis) and brain imaging of chronic pain. It also builds on older observations of altered hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal (HPA) function in fibromyalgia and related conditions, and on psychosocial models in which stress precipitates pain.

## Critiques

- Allostatic load indices combine heterogeneous biomarkers (blood pressure, metabolic, inflammatory, neuroendocrine) in different ways, and results depend on the formulation used [@sibille2017].
- Most human evidence is cross-sectional, so it cannot tell whether high allostatic load precedes pain or results from it, or from shared factors such as inactivity, poor sleep or depression.
- The framework is broad enough to accommodate almost any stress-related finding in either direction (e.g. both hyper- and hypocortisolism), which makes it hard to falsify.

## Legacy

The model has been influential in migraine research and in work linking social disadvantage, stress biology and pain, but it remains a framework with limited prospective tests. Prospective cohorts have produced conflicting results on whether stress-system dysfunction precedes chronic pain [@mcbeth2007; @generaal2016].

## Teaching notes

- Distinguish *allostasis* (adaptive) from *allostatic load* (cumulative cost) and *allostatic overload* (pathology).
- Compare with the diathesis-stress model, which addresses similar questions at the psychological level.
- Discussion prompt: what kind of prospective study would test whether allostatic load causes chronic pain?
